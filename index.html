AutoClip

AutoClip is a Windows desktop app that turns long stream recordings into short vertical clips. It listens to the stream, reads the transcript, finds the best moments, and lets you review every clip before anything is shared. Clips you approve can be sent to your own TikTok account through TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API (as drafts in your TikTok inbox).

  • Runs entirely on your own PC; no accounts or servers of ours.
  • Nothing is uploaded until you approve a clip and press Upload.
  • Your TikTok sign-in is stored encrypted on your PC only.
