AutoClip - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

AutoClip runs entirely on your own computer. We (the developers) do not run a server for it, and we do not receive, collect, store or sell your videos, clips, TikTok data or any other personal information.

What stays on your PC

  • Your videos, generated clips, transcripts, settings and review decisions are stored in your Windows user profile.
  • Your TikTok sign-in tokens and any API keys you enter are stored encrypted for your Windows user (Windows DPAPI) and are never sent to us.

What is sent to other services

  • TikTok: when you connect your account and press Upload, AutoClip talks directly to TikTok (open.tiktokapis.com) to sign you in, read your display name and open id, and upload the clips you chose. TikTok's own privacy policy applies to that data. AutoClip requests only the permissions needed for this (user.info.basic, video.upload).
  • Anthropic (optional): only if you add a Claude API key, a few still frames and transcript excerpts of candidate clips are sent to Anthropic for review.
  • Model downloads: the optional local AI model and speech models are downloaded from Hugging Face.

Your control

Disconnect TikTok in AutoClip (Settings) or revoke access in your TikTok account settings at any time. Deleting AutoClip's data folder (%APPDATA%\AutoClip) removes everything it stored.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/saberapexyt-commits/autoclip-legal/issues.
